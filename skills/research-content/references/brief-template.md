(one page). Real sources only — if a claim has no source, cut it.

## WORKED EXAMPLE (shape + depth to match)

```
# Research Brief: AI follow-up never forgets a prospect

**The idea (one line):** Most reps lose prospects to forgotten follow-up; an AI
agent that follows up for you fixes the #1 leak in the funnel.
**Pillar:** Make your team more effective
**Team-first angle:** Give every leader's team a follow-up system so no warm
contact goes cold while they sleep.

## What this actually is
An AI follow-up agent is software that messages your contacts on a schedule, in
your voice, and surfaces who's ready to talk — so the rep only steps in for the
real conversations. It is not a chatbot on a website; it works the rep's own
warm list across text/email.

## Why it matters right now
1. The fortune is in the follow-up, and most reps stop too early — <source on
   avg. touches-to-conversion>.
2. AI messaging quality crossed the "sounds human" line in the last year —
   <source: model release / date>.
3. Reps are time-starved; the follow-up is the first thing dropped — <source>.

## What most people get wrong  (THE HOOK)
People think AI replaces the relationship. It does the opposite — it keeps the
relationship alive long enough for the human moment to happen.

## Three key points
1. Claim: Speed-to-lead + persistence win, not charisma | Example: <named study/
   stat> | Takeaway: The system that follows up beats the rep who forgets.
2. Claim: It runs while you sleep | Example: <concrete> | Takeaway: Your team's
   pipeline doesn't clock out.
3. Claim: It's duplicable | Example: <concrete> | Takeaway: A new rep inherits the
   follow-up engine on day one.

## Brent's angle
39 years of working a tickler file by hand — the rolodex nobody actually works.
Tiger is that discipline, handed to an agent. Ties to: build your own AI-powered
distribution org.

## Sources
- <real URL>
- <real URL>

## Compliance flags
No income claims — frame as a follow-up/leverage benefit, not "make more money."
```

Note: bracketed `<source>` placeholders in the example are reminders to fill with
**real, found** data during research — never ship a brief with an invented stat.
